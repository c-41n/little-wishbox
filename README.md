🎄 Little Wishbox | 小小愿望盒
A fun Christmas gift-drawing game for couples, friends, colleagues, and family.

一个适合情侣、朋友、同事和家人一起玩的圣诞礼物抽签小游戏。

🎁 About the Project | 项目介绍
Little Wishbox is a simple web-based game that lets users write down their wishes and share a personalized drawing link with others.

Little Wishbox 是一个简单的网页小游戏，让用户写下自己的愿望，并生成专属抽签链接与他人分享。

✨ How to Play | 游戏玩法
Step 1 第一步：Write down your wishes.
写下你的不同等级愿望。

Step 2 第二步：Generate your personalized link.
生成专属分享链接。

Step 3 第三步：Share the link with someone you want to play with.
将链接分享给想一起玩的人。
Let them draw and reveal a wish!
让对方抽签，揭晓抽中的愿望！

🌐 Built With | 使用技术

CSS

🎅 Purpose | 项目初衷

A little festive project made to bring more fun, surprises, and joy to Christmas gift exchanges.

一个为圣诞礼物交换增添乐趣、惊喜与快乐的小小创作。

⚠️ Privacy Notice | 隐私提醒

Wish information is encoded in the sharing URL and is not encrypted. Please avoid including passwords or sensitive personal information.

愿望内容会编码在分享链接中，但并未加密。请勿填写密码或其他敏感个人资料。
